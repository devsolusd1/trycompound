Pools that only get deeper.

Compound is a Solana launchpad built on Meteora. Coins that fill their curve graduate into a Meteora pool that compounds: it puts most of its fee back in, with its liquidity locked for good.

Scroll to follow one coin

It starts on a curve.

Each coin opens on a bonding curve at about $5k of market cap. Anyone can buy or sell on it, with a 1% fee.

The curve is set in SOL, so the dollar figures move a little with SOL's price.

Then it graduates.

When the curve fills, at about $25k, the coin moves into a Meteora DAMM v2 pool, and all of its liquidity is locked there for good.

Nobody can take it out: not the creator, not Compound.

Every trade feeds the pool.

Putting the fee back in is native to Meteora: DAMM v2 pools have a compounding mode, where the pool itself adds the fee to its reserves on every trade, with no bot in between. Compound turns it on for every coin.

Each trade pays 2%. Every tile that falls in is one trade's 2%, and it breaks like this:

  • 64% goes back into the pool, in SOL
  • 16% buys back and burns the Compound token
  • 20% is Meteora's protocol fee

Meteora takes 20% of the 2%. Of the LP fee left, 80% goes back into the pool's reserves and 20% buys back and burns Compound.

The tiles are a drawing, not live trades. Press the atom to drop one in.

The floor only rises.

The floor is what the pool would still hold if every holder sold everything. Each trade adds to it, and the liquidity can't leave, so it never falls.

The price still can. It moves with buys and sells, like any coin's.

Devnet test: 10 round trips worth about 6.4 times the SOL in the pool raised the floor by 3.6%. It ran with a 1% pool fee; Compound pools charge 2%.

Every graduated coin joins the compound.

It is bonded to one token, Compound. The violet 16% of every trade's fee in its pool buys Compound back and burns it, so the more coins trade, the more flows to the middle.

The molecule is a drawing, not live coins: no real coin has launched yet. Coins don't share liquidity; each pool stays its own. Compound claims each pool's 16% in batches, makes the buy and burns what it bought. So far that has run only on devnet, with test coins.
